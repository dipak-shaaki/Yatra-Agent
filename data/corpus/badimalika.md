# Badimalika

A major Hindu Shakti Peeth (sacred site) dedicated to Goddess Bhagwati, located at 4,200m in Bajura district, far-western Nepal. According to Hindu mythology, this is where the left shoulder of Goddess Sati fell. The pagoda-style temple sits atop a ridge surrounded by 22 rolling alpine meadows (patans), with views of Mt. Api and Mt. Saipal — one of Nepal's most remote and least-explored pilgrimage destinations.

## Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) brings steady weather and blooming rhododendrons/wildflowers. Autumn (September–November) offers the clearest mountain views. **Janai Purnima** (the full moon of Shrawan, around August) is the major pilgrimage festival, drawing thousands of visitors despite difficult monsoon trekking conditions. Winter brings heavy snowfall, making the high areas inaccessible.

## Route and Access

The trailhead is Martadi, Bajura's district headquarters:
- Flight from Kathmandu to Dhangadhi or Nepalgunj, then road/jeep to Martadi (approximately 10–12 hours)
- Alternative: flight to Dhangadhi/Nepalgunj to Sanfebagar (Achham), then bus to Bajura

From Martadi, it's a 2–4 day trek on foot to reach the temple, passing Budha Krodh and Triveni Dham (a sacred river confluence at 3,870m).

## Difficulty

Rated moderate to strenuous. This is a remote, high-altitude trek with limited lodges and facilities — travelers need to carry their own supplies. A licensed guide is mandatory under Nepal Tourism Board regulations as of 2026.

## Duration

Roughly 10–12 days total from Kathmandu including travel; the trekking portion from Martadi alone takes 2–4 days.

## Permits

- **Khaptad National Park permit** (approx USD 15) — relevant on some route variants
- **Bajura local area permit** (approx USD 15)

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 20,000–35,000 per person**, covering domestic flight to Dhangadhi/Nepalgunj, jeep transport to Martadi, guide, basic lodging or camping, and meals. This is a rough domestic-traveler estimate — flight cost is the single biggest factor and fluctuates seasonally. Foreign-tourist package prices quoted online (USD 1,000-2,500) reflect fully organized international tours and are not representative of independent domestic travel costs.

## Culture Notes

The trek offers insight into Khas culture and the traditional lifestyle of the Bajura and Doti districts. Pilgrims traditionally bathe at Triveni Dham before ascending, believed to wash away sins.
