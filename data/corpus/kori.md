# Kori (Kori Danda)

A newly popularized, off-the-beaten-path trek in the Annapurna Conservation Area near Sikles village, often described as the "center point of Nepal." The trek offers alpine meadow scenery, strong Gurung culture, and views of Annapurna II and Lamjung Himal, with a side visit possible to Kapuche Lake, a glacial lake. It's increasingly popular among domestic tourists seeking a quieter alternative to ABC and Mardi Himal.

## Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) brings rhododendron blooms. Autumn (September–November) offers crisp air and clear mountain visibility. Winter is trekkable with snowy alpine scenery, but nights can drop below 0°C.

## Route and Access

The trailhead is at Sikles village, reached via Pokhara:
- **Kathmandu to Pokhara**: flight or drive (6–7 hours)
- **Pokhara to Sikles**: off-road jeep drive

## Difficulty

Rated moderate — well suited to beginners seeking solitude rather than technical challenge. Expect 5–7 hours of walking per day. The highest point, Kori Danda, sits around 3,600–3,800m. Altitude sickness risk is relatively low since overnight stops remain below many high-altitude routes, though nights can be cold.

## Duration

Approximately 6 days.

## Permits

- **Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP)** — NPR 3,000 for foreign nationals; Nepali citizens generally pay a much lower or no fee, confirm current rate at the ACAP office
- **TIMS card**

## Culture Notes

The trail passes through Sikles and Tangting, among Nepal's largest Gurung communities, many with strong Gurkha (British/Indian Army) heritage. Homestay-based accommodation offers direct cultural engagement with local families.

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 8,000–14,000 per person** for the 6-day trek, covering homestay/teahouse lodging, meals, and jeep transport to/from Sikles. This is a rough domestic-traveler estimate, not an official figure.

## Practical Notes

Mobile network coverage is limited — NTC works in Sikles and parts of the route to Tangting, but there is no coverage at higher points like Hugu Goth or Kori Danda itself.
