# Mardi Himal

A short, quieter trek in the Annapurna region, east of the main ABC trail, ending near Mardi Himal Base Camp/Viewpoint beneath Machhapuchhre (Fishtail). The trail only opened for teahouse trekking in 2012 and offers close-up Himalayan views with far fewer crowds than ABC or Everest Base Camp — a good choice for a short, beginner-friendly Himalayan trip.

## Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) brings blooming rhododendron forests. Autumn/post-monsoon (September–November/December) offers the most stable, clear weather and is the main tourist season. Winter is trekkable to lower points but colder and less predictable.

## Route and Access

The trailhead is at Kande or Dhampus, reached via Pokhara:
- **Kathmandu to Pokhara**: flight (~25 minutes) or drive (6–7 hours) via Prithvi Highway
- **Pokhara to Kande/Dhampus**: short 1–1.5 hour drive

## Difficulty

One of the more beginner-friendly treks in Nepal, rated easy to moderate. Expect 4–7 hours of walking per day depending on route, with some steep stone-step sections closer to High Camp (3,660m). No technical climbing skills are required to reach High Camp or the main viewpoint. A guide is not compulsory but is recommended, especially in winter or for solo trekkers.

## Duration

4–7 days depending on route and pace. Shorter options starting from Sidhing village or Landruk cut 1–2 days off the standard itinerary.

## Permits

- **Annapurna Conservation Area Permit (ACAP)** — NPR 3,000 for foreign nationals; Nepali citizens generally pay a much lower or no fee, confirm current rate at the ACAP office
- **TIMS card**

> **Note:** ACAP is consistently required on this route, but sources disagree on current TIMS enforcement on Annapurna-area trails as of 2026 — some report it abolished or unchecked, others list it as active. Confirm current requirements with the Nepal Tourism Board or a trekking agency before departure.

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 8,000–15,000 per person** for a 4-7 day trek, covering teahouse lodging, meals, and transport from Pokhara. This is a domestic-traveler estimate — foreign-tourist all-inclusive guided packages (commonly quoted around USD 420-490) are priced very differently and not representative of independent domestic travel costs.

## Culture Notes

The trail passes through Gurung villages including Dhampus, and is less commercially developed than the main Annapurna Base Camp route.
