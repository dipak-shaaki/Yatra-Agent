# Panauti

One of Nepal's oldest and best-preserved medieval towns, sitting at the confluence of the Roshi and Punyamati rivers (known as Tribeni), about 32km southeast of Kathmandu. Home to around 40 temples, including the Indreshwar Mahadev Temple (dating to 1294 and believed to be the oldest surviving wooden temple in Nepal). Panauti retains well-preserved Newari architecture and a slower pace of life, with far fewer tourists than Bhaktapur or Patan.

## Best Time to Visit

A good year-round day trip, with spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) offering the best weather. Panauti hosts 28 different festivals across the year; visiting during a festival — such as the bi-monthly aarti puja — adds cultural richness. Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain.

## Route and Access

- Local bus from Ratna Park bus park in Kathmandu, approximately 1–1.5 hours, low cost
- Private car/taxi, faster and more flexible
- An alternative scenic arrival route: hike from Sanga (approximately 10km, easy-moderate difficulty)

## Difficulty

Easy — a walkable heritage town suitable for all visitors.

## Duration

Half-day to a full day trip; 2 nights recommended if doing a community homestay.

## Things to Do

- Self-guided heritage walk through the old town and the Tribeni river confluence
- Visit Indreshwar Mahadev Temple and the adjoining Panauti Museum
- Visit Brahmayani Temple (17th century)
- Day hike to Namo Buddha, a Tibetan Buddhist pilgrimage site with a monastery, about a 250m uphill climb from Panauti
- Bike tour through surrounding villages, orange farms, and Khopasi
- Community homestay experience with local Newari families

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 1,500–4,000 per person** for a day trip (local bus fare and meals), or **NPR 4,000–7,000** if staying overnight at a community homestay. This is a rough domestic-traveler estimate, not an official figure.

## Culture Notes

Panauti is often described as a commuter town relatively untouched by mass tourism, offering an authentic alternative to more-visited Newari heritage towns like Bhaktapur.
