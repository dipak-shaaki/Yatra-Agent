# Rara Lake

Nepal's largest lake (also called Mahendra Tal), covering 10.8 sq km at 2,990m within Rara National Park in the remote far-northwest. The lake's water shifts color through the day — turquoise in the morning, midnight blue at noon, orange-red at sunset — and it remains one of the quietest, least-visited natural attractions in Nepal despite its scale. Rara National Park hosts over 214 bird species and mammals including musk deer, Himalayan black bear, red panda, and snow leopard.

## Best Time to Visit

Spring (March–May) offers clear weather and melting-ice greenery. Autumn (September–November) brings the clearest mountain views and moderate temperatures. Winter can bring heavy snowfall and very cold conditions; monsoon (June–August) brings rain and slippery trails.

## Route and Access

- **Fastest route**: fly Kathmandu to Nepalgunj (~1 hour), then Nepalgunj to Talcha airport near Rara (~35–40 min), then a 2–4 hour hike from Talcha to the lake
- **Overland/budget route**: Kathmandu to Surkhet by bus/jeep (13–15 hours), then Surkhet to Jumla via rugged roads (8–10 hours), then trek from Jumla to Rara (approximately 50km, about 3 days)

## Difficulty

Rated moderate. The trek stays below 4,000m, making it more accessible than high-altitude treks in the Everest or Annapurna regions, and suitable for beginners in good health. The main challenges are remoteness and limited infrastructure rather than altitude.

## Duration

10–15 days round trip from Kathmandu depending on the route chosen; significantly shorter with the fly-in option.

## Permits

- **TIMS card** (NPR 2,000 / approx USD 15 for foreigners, NPR 1,000 / approx USD 8 for SAARC nationals — domestic Nepali citizens should confirm the current requirement with the Nepal Tourism Board)
- **Rara National Park entry permit**

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 25,000–45,000 per person** using the fly-in route (Kathmandu-Nepalgunj-Talcha), covering flights, basic lodging, and meals. The overland route (via Surkhet and Jumla) can cost significantly less but adds many extra travel days. This is a rough domestic-traveler estimate, not an official figure — flight prices fluctuate and should be checked directly with airlines.
