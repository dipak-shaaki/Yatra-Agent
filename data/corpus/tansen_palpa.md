# Tansen (Palpa)

A historic Newari-Magar hill town at 1,310m, perched above the Kali Gandaki River between Butwal and Pokhara. Tansen was once the capital of the powerful Tansen (Sen) kingdom before the rise of the Shah dynasty. Known for cobbled medieval streets, traditional Dhaka fabric weaving, bronze karuwa water pots, coffee farming, and views of Dhaulagiri and Annapurna from Shreenagar Hill — quieter and less touristy than Bandipur.

## Best Time to Visit

A year-round destination, with the clearest mountain views typically October–December and March–April. Winter months (December–February) bring atmospheric mist over the valley. Monsoon (June–August) brings heavy rain and humidity.

## Route and Access

There is no direct public transport from Kathmandu:
- Take a bus to Butwal, then a shared jeep/bus to Tansen (approximately 1.5 hours from Butwal)
- Private taxi available for direct hire from Kathmandu
- From Pokhara: approximately 122 km, around 4 hours via Syangja
- Nearest airport: Gautam Buddha International Airport, Bhairahwa

## Difficulty

Easy — a walkable town with short, optional hikes to nearby viewpoints.

## Duration

1–2 days is generally sufficient.

## Things to Do

- Walk the old bazaar's cobbled streets and Newari architecture
- Hike to Shreenagar Hill and its View Tower for sunrise/sunset and Himalayan views (Dhaulagiri, Annapurna)
- Day trip to Rani Mahal (the "Taj Mahal of Nepal"), an 1894 riverside palace at the confluence of the Ridi and Kali Gandaki rivers
- Visit a local coffee farm
- Shop for Dhaka fabric and bronze karuwa water pots
- Try local Palpali food, including hansko chhyola

## Estimated Budget

Roughly **NPR 3,000–6,000 per person per day**, covering bus fare via Butwal, guesthouse lodging, and meals. This is a rough domestic-traveler estimate for a 1-2 day trip, not an official figure.

## Culture Notes

Home to a mix of Magar and Newar communities. Newar traders historically settled here at a key trade crossroads, bringing distinctive architecture and craft traditions with them.
